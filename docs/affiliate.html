How it works

  1. Create or sign in to your eCuisine account.
  2. Activate your affiliate profile and copy your unique link.
  3. Share the link with friends, customers, social followers or your community.
  4. When a customer places an order through your link, the order appears in your dashboard.
  5. Commission is calculated on product subtotal, excluding delivery. It becomes payable as soon as eCuisine marks the order delivered. eCuisine pays affiliates on Fridays and records the bank transfer in the payout history.

Please promote honestly, do not place orders through your own link, and do not promise delivery or payment terms on behalf of eCuisine. Attribution depends on the customer using the same browser where the referral link was first opened.
